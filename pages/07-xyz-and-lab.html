07 — XYZ & Lab

RGB is great for screens, but for measurement and comparison we often use CIE XYZ and CIELAB.

Big idea: XYZ is a standard “hub” space, and Lab is a perceptual-ish space where distances relate more to what people notice.
  • XYZ: a “universal” way to describe color.
  • Lab: a way where “how far apart” colors are often matches “how different they look”.
  • ΔE: a number that says “how different are two colors?”
  • We convert sRGB → linear RGB → XYZ → Lab (D65 white point).
  • Then we compute ΔE76 between two Lab colors.

This uses the standard sRGB matrix and D65 white reference. ΔE76 is Euclidean distance in Lab; better formulas exist (ΔE00), but ΔE76 is a clean first step.
