07 — CIEXYZ & CIELAB

RGB is a screen control system. CIEXYZ is a measurement-like bridge, and CIELAB is a more “perception-friendly” space for comparing colors.

Big idea: XYZ and Lab help us talk about color in ways that connect better to human vision and to printing workflows.

Why do we need XYZ and Lab?

  • RGB says “how much light a screen uses.”
  • XYZ is a “universal language” for color measurements.
  • Lab helps describe colors in a way that matches how we *feel* differences.

Where XYZ and Lab show up

  • XYZ: a device-independent connector between systems.
  • Lab: a space where distances can roughly match perceived differences (better than RGB).
  • Printing: profiles often use Lab/XYZ internally to translate colors from screen → paper.

Tristimulus + reference white

XYZ is tied to the CIE standard observer (color matching functions). Lab is defined relative to a reference white (here we’ll use D65), and applies a non-linear transform to better align with perception.

Lab is not perfect (and “uniformity” depends on conditions), but it’s a major step up from RGB for comparisons.
